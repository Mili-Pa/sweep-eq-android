Mr. Calibrator - third-party notices

1) AutoEq (MIT License)

The equalization-curve shaping in this app (smoothing with a wide treble window, slope limiting with
peak/dip protection, treble gain factor, the sharpness limit for peaking filters and the fixed-band
graphic-EQ fitting) follows the approach of AutoEq, https://github.com/jaakkopasanen/AutoEq.
The headphone target curves (Harman Over-Ear 2018, Harman Over-Ear 2015, Harman In-Ear 2019 and its less-bass
variant, Harman Over-Ear 2018 less-bass variant, ISO 11904-1 and B&K 5128 diffuse field, oratory1990 In-Ear and
Optimum Hi-Fi, AutoEq In-Ear, InnerFidelity 2017) were sampled from the target files in that repository.

2) HouseCurve target tables

The room and car target tables (B&K, the Toole-inspired Harman in-room tilt, Car A, Car B and Car C) are numeric
curve tables published by HouseCurve, https://housecurve.com (Greg Wilding), based on the B&K publication 17-197,
Dr. Floyd Toole's work, the JBL curve and a Crutchfield curve. Only the numbers are used.

4) Reference curves and standards

The X-curve (ISO 2969 / SMPTE) is a published standard. Only numeric values describing its shape are used here;
no text of the standards is reproduced. The sound-group frequency ranges use the common audio-engineering band
names, and the speech band is the ITU-T G.712 telephone band (300-3400 Hz). Names of products and companies belong
to their owners.
